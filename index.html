Who We Are

RandDloc is developed and provided by RandDloc. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app. For privacy questions, contact caimingkai229@outlook.com.

Information Stored on Your Device

The app saves the names you enter, draw settings, group count, names already drawn, and your latest 200 results in its local app storage. This information is used to run draws, make groups, shuffle names, restore your settings, and display history. Names can be personal information; enter only information you are authorized to use.

What We Receive

Core features work offline. The app does not send your names, results, settings, or usage activity to RandDloc. It has no accounts, advertising, tracking, analytics SDKs, or developer-operated cloud storage. We do not sell your information or share locally stored app data with advertisers or data brokers.

Support Emails and External Links

If you choose to email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in the message. We use this information to respond to your request and address the issue. Email is handled by your chosen email service and our Outlook email service under their respective policies. Do not include sensitive information unless it is necessary for your request. Opening Apple's license link takes you to Apple's website; core app features do not require it.

Retention and Deletion

Local names and settings remain until you change them or delete the app. History retains only the latest 200 results, with older results removed as new ones are generated. To remove saved names, open Your Names, tap Clear Names, and then Save. To delete results that may still contain those names, open History and confirm Clear History. Clearing names alone does not delete history. Reset Pool clears the record of names already drawn. Deleting the app, rather than offloading it, removes its local app data. We keep support correspondence only as needed to resolve your request or meet applicable legal obligations; you can email us to request its deletion, subject to those obligations.

Apple Services and Device Backups

The app does not implement cloud sync. Depending on your device settings, Apple or your computer may include app data in device backups. Backup retention and deletion are managed through those services. Apple may separately process App Store or diagnostic information under your Apple settings and Apple's privacy policy.

Permissions and Security

The app does not request access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, location, or advertising identifier. Local app storage is protected by the security features of iOS. Protect your device and backups; no storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Children's Information

The app does not ask for age, create child accounts, or transmit entered names to us. Parents, educators, and other users should avoid entering unnecessary personal or sensitive information about children. If a support message raises a concern about a child's information, contact us so we can address it.

Changes and Contact

We will update this policy and its effective date if our data practices change. You can read the current policy in About at any time. Direct privacy questions or requests to RandDloc at caimingkai229@outlook.com.
